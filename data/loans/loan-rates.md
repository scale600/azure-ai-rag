# Loan Rates and APR

Interest rates vary by product, term, and creditworthiness.

## Current Rate Ranges
- Personal loans: 7.99% to 23.99% APR
- Auto loans (new): 5.49% to 12.99% APR
- Home loans: 6.25% to 8.75% APR (subject to daily market rates)

## Fixed vs Variable
- Personal and auto loans are always fixed rate
- Home loans may be fixed or adjustable

## Rate Discounts
- 0.25% discount with automatic payment from our checking account
- 0.50% discount for relationship banking customers ($25,000+ combined balance)

## How Rates Are Determined
Your rate is based on credit score, income, debt-to-income ratio, loan amount, and term. Checking your rate does not affect your credit score.
