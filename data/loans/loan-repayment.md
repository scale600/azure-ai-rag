# Loan Repayment and Payment Options

Monthly payments are fixed for the life of the loan.

## Payment Methods
- Automatic debit from checking account
- Online bill pay
- Mail a check
- In-branch payment

## Payment Allocation
Payments apply first to accrued interest, then to principal.

## Late Payments
- Grace period: 10 days
- Late fee: $25 or 5% of payment, whichever is less
- Payments more than 30 days late are reported to credit bureaus

## Extra Payments
Make extra principal payments anytime with no penalty. Extra payments reduce total interest and shorten the loan term.
