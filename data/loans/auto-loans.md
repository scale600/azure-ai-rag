# Auto Loan Overview

Auto loans finance the purchase of new or used vehicles.

## Loan Details
- New vehicles: up to 100% financing, terms 36-84 months
- Used vehicles: up to 90% financing, terms 36-72 months
- Fixed APR: 5.49% to 15.99% based on credit and vehicle age

## Requirements
- Vehicle must be less than 10 years old
- Comprehensive insurance required
- Title held until loan payoff

## Pre-Approval
Get pre-approved before visiting a dealer to strengthen your negotiating position. Pre-approvals are valid for 60 days.

## Early Payoff
No prepayment penalty. Pay off the loan anytime without additional fees.
