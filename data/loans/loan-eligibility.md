# Loan Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for a loan, you must meet our basic eligibility criteria.

## General Requirements
- Be at least 18 years old
- Have a valid government-issued ID
- Have a verifiable source of income
- Have a U.S. residential address

## Credit Requirements
- Minimum credit score: 580 for most personal loans
- Higher scores receive lower rates
- No minimum for secured loans

## Income Verification
- Pay stubs for the last 30 days
- Tax returns for the last 2 years (self-employed)
- Bank statements for the last 3 months

## Documentation
You will need to upload supporting documents during the application. Incomplete applications are delayed until all documents are received.
