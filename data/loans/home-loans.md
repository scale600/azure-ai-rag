# Home Loan (Mortgage) Overview

Home loans help finance the purchase or refinance of a home.

## Loan Types
- Fixed-rate: 15 or 30 years, stable monthly payment
- Adjustable-rate (ARM): lower initial rate, adjusts after fixed period
- FHA/VA: government-backed options with lower down payments

## Down Payment
- Conventional: as low as 3% for qualified borrowers
- FHA: as low as 3.5%
- VA: 0% for eligible veterans

## Requirements
- Minimum credit score: 620
- Debt-to-income ratio generally below 43%
- Proof of income and assets

## Process
Pre-approval, home search, offer, underwriting, closing. Typical timeline is 30-45 days from application to closing.
