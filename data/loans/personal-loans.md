# Personal Loan Overview

Personal loans provide a fixed amount of money repaid in fixed monthly installments.

## Loan Details
- Amount range: $1,000 to $50,000
- Terms: 12 to 60 months
- Fixed APR: 7.99% to 23.99% based on creditworthiness
- No origination fee, no prepayment penalty

## Use Cases
- Debt consolidation
- Home improvement
- Major purchases
- Unexpected expenses

## Repayment
Monthly payments are fixed and auto-debited from your checking account. Payments include both principal and interest.

## Application
Apply online in minutes. Most decisions are instant, with funds deposited within 1-2 business days of approval.
