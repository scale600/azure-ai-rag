# Account Fees

Review common fees associated with deposit accounts.

## Monthly Maintenance Fees
- Standard checking: $10 (waivable)
- Premium checking: $12 (waivable)
- Savings: $5 (waivable with $300 balance)

## Service Fees
- Paper statement: $3 per month
- Cashier's check: $10
- Money order: $5
- Account research: $25 per hour

## Waiver Conditions
Most monthly fees are waived with minimum balances or direct deposit. See the fee waiver policy for details.

## Avoiding Fees
Use electronic statements, maintain minimum balances, and set up direct deposit to avoid most fees.
