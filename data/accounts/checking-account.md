# Checking Account Overview

A checking account is your primary account for everyday spending and bill payments.

## Features
- Debit card with contactless payment
- Online and mobile banking
- Bill pay service
- Direct deposit

## Fees
- Monthly fee: $0 with direct deposit or $1,500 minimum balance, otherwise $10
- Overdraft: $35 per item (see overdraft policy)

## Opening Requirements
- Government-issued ID
- Social Security number
- Minimum opening deposit: $25
- Proof of address

## Benefits
- Free in-network ATM access
- Mobile check deposit
- Account alerts via text and email
