# Savings Account Overview

A savings account helps you earn interest while keeping funds accessible.

## Interest Rates
- Standard savings: 0.01% APY
- High-yield savings: 4.00% APY (requires $500 minimum balance)

## Fees
- Monthly fee: $0 with $300 minimum balance, otherwise $5
- Excessive withdrawals (more than 6 per month): $10 each

## Features
- Automatic transfers from checking
- Round-up savings
- FDIC insured up to $250,000

## Withdrawals
Federal regulation limits certain withdrawals to 6 per month. Transfers within our institution are unlimited.
