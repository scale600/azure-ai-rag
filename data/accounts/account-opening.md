# How to Open an Account

Opening an account takes about 10 minutes online or in a branch.

## Online Application
1. Visit our website and select "Open an Account"
2. Enter your personal information
3. Verify your identity
4. Fund your account with an initial deposit

## Required Documents
- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport, or state ID)
- Social Security number
- Proof of address (utility bill or lease, if ID doesn't match)

## Funding Options
- Transfer from an existing bank account
- Debit or credit card
- Cash or check at a branch

## Approval
Most applications are approved instantly. Some may require additional review within 1-2 business days.
