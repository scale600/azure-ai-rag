# Minimum Balance Requirements

Minimum balance requirements determine whether monthly fees are waived.

## Checking Accounts
- Standard checking: $1,500 minimum daily balance OR $500 monthly direct deposit
- Premium checking: $10,000 combined balance OR $25,000 across all accounts

## Savings Accounts
- Standard savings: $300 minimum daily balance
- High-yield savings: $500 minimum daily balance

## How Balance Is Calculated
The minimum daily balance is the lowest balance in your account on any day of the statement cycle.

## Falling Below Minimum
If you fall below the minimum, the monthly fee applies for that statement cycle. One-month grace is offered for first-time occurrences.
