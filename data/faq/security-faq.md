# Security FAQ

How we protect your account and how you can help.

## How do you protect my account?
We use encryption, multi-factor authentication, fraud monitoring, and automatic sign-out to protect your information.

## What is multi-factor authentication?
When you log in from a new device, we send a one-time code to your phone or email. You must enter this code to complete login.

## How do I recognize phishing?
We never ask for your password or full Social Security number by email or phone. If a message seems suspicious, don't click links — contact us directly.

## What should I do if I suspect fraud?
Immediately call 1-800-555-0199 or lock your card in the mobile app. Report unauthorized transactions within 60 days.

## How do I report a lost or stolen card?
Lock your card in the app, then call us to request a replacement. See the lost card policy for details.
