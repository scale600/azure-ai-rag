# General FAQ

Answers to our most common questions.

## How do I contact customer service?
Call 1-800-555-0199, available 24/7. You can also send a secure message through online banking or visit a branch.

## What are your branch hours?
Most branches are open Monday through Friday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Saturday 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Some locations vary.

## How do I reset my online banking password?
Select "Forgot Password" on the login screen and follow the prompts. You'll need your username and access to your registered email or phone.

## Is my money insured?
Yes. Deposits are FDIC-insured up to $250,000 per depositor, per account ownership category.

## How do I set up direct deposit?
Provide your employer with your account number and routing number, found on your checks or in online banking.
