# Mobile App FAQ

Answers about our mobile banking app.

## How do I download the app?
Search for our app in the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. The app is free to download.

## What can I do in the app?
- Check balances and transaction history
- Deposit checks using mobile deposit
- Transfer money and pay bills
- Lock or unlock your debit card
- Set up account alerts

## How does mobile check deposit work?
Select "Deposit", endorse the check, and photograph the front and back. Funds are usually available within 1 business day.

## Is the app secure?
Yes. The app uses the same encryption and authentication as online banking, plus biometric login (fingerprint or face ID).
