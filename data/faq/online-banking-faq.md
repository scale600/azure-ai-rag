# Online Banking FAQ

Common questions about managing accounts online.

## How do I enroll in online banking?
Visit the website, click "Enroll", and verify your identity with your account number and Social Security number. You'll create a username and password.

## Can I pay bills online?
Yes. Bill Pay lets you schedule one-time or recurring payments. Most payments arrive in 2-3 business days.

## How do I view my statements?
Statements are available in the "Documents" section. You can view or download up to 7 years of statements.

## How do I transfer money between accounts?
Use "Transfers" in online banking. Transfers between your accounts are instant and free.

## Is online banking secure?
Yes. We use encryption, multi-factor authentication, and fraud monitoring to protect your information.
