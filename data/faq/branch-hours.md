# Branch Hours and Locations

We operate branches across the region for in-person service.

## Standard Hours
- Monday through Friday: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM - 1:00 PM
- Sunday: Closed

## Holiday Closures
Branches are closed on federal holidays, including New Year's Day, Independence Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas.

## Finding a Branch
Use the branch locator on our website or mobile app to find the nearest location, hours, and services offered.

## Drive-Through and ATM
Many locations offer drive-through service and 24/7 ATMs. ATMs are available even when the branch is closed.

## Appointments
For complex services like loans or safe deposit boxes, schedule an appointment to reduce wait time.
