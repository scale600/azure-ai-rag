# Address Verification

We may require proof of your residential address in certain situations.

## When Address Verification Is Required
- Your ID shows a different address than your application
- You recently moved
- Electronic verification could not confirm your address

## Accepted Documents
- Utility bill (electric, water, gas) within 60 days
- Lease or rental agreement
- Mortgage statement
- Bank or credit card statement

## How to Submit
Upload documents securely through online banking or email to verification@examplefinancial.com.

## Processing Time
Documents are typically reviewed within 1 business day. You will be notified by email once verified.
