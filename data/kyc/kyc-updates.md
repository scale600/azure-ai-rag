# Updating Your Information (KYC Updates)

Keep your personal information current to avoid service interruptions.

## What to Update
- Legal name change (marriage, divorce)
- Address change
- New identification documents
- Citizenship status changes

## How to Update
1. Online: log in and update your profile
2. Branch: visit with your new documents
3. Mail: submit a signed change form with copies of documents

## Required Documents
- Name change: marriage certificate or court order
- Address change: utility bill or lease

## Processing Time
Online updates apply immediately. Document-based changes take 1-2 business days to verify.
