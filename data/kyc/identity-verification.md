# Identity Verification (KYC)

Federal regulations require us to verify your identity when you open an account.

## What We Verify
- Full legal name
- Date of birth
- Social Security number
- Residential address

## Verification Methods
1. Electronic verification against credit bureaus (instant, most common)
2. Document upload (if electronic verification fails)
3. In-person verification at a branch

## Accepted Documents
- Driver's license or state ID
- Passport
- Permanent resident card
- Military ID

## Why Verification Matters
Identity verification helps prevent fraud, money laundering, and identity theft. Your information is protected and used only for compliance purposes.
