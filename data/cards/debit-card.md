# Debit Card Overview

Your debit card provides convenient access to your checking account.

## Features
- Contactless payment
- Mobile wallet support (Apple Pay, Google Pay)
- 24/7 fraud monitoring
- Instant card lock via mobile app

## Daily Limits
- Purchases: $2,500 per day
- ATM withdrawals: $500 per day
- Limits can be adjusted by request

## Fees
- In-network ATM: free
- Out-of-network ATM: $3
- Foreign transaction: 1%

## Replacement
Lost or stolen cards are replaced free of charge within 5-7 business days, or next business day for a fee.
