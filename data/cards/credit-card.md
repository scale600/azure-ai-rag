# Credit Card Overview

Our credit cards offer rewards and purchasing power.

## Card Options
- Cash Back card: 1.5% cash back on all purchases, no annual fee
- Travel card: 2x points on travel and dining, $95 annual fee
- Secured card: build credit with a refundable deposit

## Key Terms
- APR: 17.99% to 26.99% variable based on creditworthiness
- Grace period: 25 days on purchases when you pay in full
- Late fee: up to $40

## Rewards
Cash back and points are earned on eligible purchases. Rewards can be redeemed for statement credits, travel, or gift cards.

## Applying
Apply online. Approval is based on credit history and income. Secured cards require a deposit equal to the credit limit.
