# Disputing a Transaction

Dispute unauthorized or incorrect transactions within 60 days.

## When to Dispute
- Unauthorized charges you didn't make
- Incorrect amounts
- Goods or services not received
- Duplicate charges

## How to Dispute
1. Log in to online banking and select the transaction
2. Choose "Dispute"
3. Provide the reason and supporting details
4. Submit

## Investigation
We investigate within 10 business days and provisionally credit your account during the investigation. Final resolution typically within 45-90 days.

## What to Expect
You will be notified of the outcome by mail or email. If the dispute is resolved in your favor, the credit becomes permanent.
