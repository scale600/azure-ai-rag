# Card Activation

Activate your new debit or credit card before use.

## How to Activate
1. Call the activation number on the card sticker
2. Or log in to the mobile app and select "Activate Card"
3. Or activate through online banking

## Information Needed
- Card number
- Last 4 digits of your Social Security number (or other verification)

## Setting a PIN
For debit cards, set your PIN during activation. You can change your PIN at any in-network ATM.

## Common Issues
If activation fails, confirm you are entering the correct card number and that the card has not expired. Contact support if the problem continues.
