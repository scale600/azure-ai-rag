# Lost or Stolen Card

Report a lost or stolen card immediately to prevent unauthorized use.

## Immediate Steps
1. Lock your card in the mobile app (instant)
2. Report the card by calling 1-800-555-0199
3. Review recent transactions for unauthorized activity

## Replacement
- Standard replacement: free, arrives in 5-7 business days
- Expedited replacement: $30, arrives in 1-2 business days

## Your Liability
If you report a lost card before unauthorized use, you are not liable. If reported within 2 business days, your liability is limited to $50.

## Temporary Access
While waiting for your replacement, you can use mobile wallet or visit a branch with ID to withdraw cash.
