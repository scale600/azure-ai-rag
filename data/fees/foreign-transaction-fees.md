# Foreign Transaction Fees

A foreign transaction fee applies to purchases made outside the United States or in a foreign currency.

## Fee Details
- Rate: 1% of the transaction amount
- Applies to: debit and credit card purchases, ATM withdrawals abroad
- Currency conversion: performed at the network exchange rate

## Cards Without Foreign Transaction Fees
- Premium checking customers: 0% on debit cards
- Travel credit card: 0% on purchases

## Tips
- Notify us of international travel to avoid declined transactions
- Prefer local currency when prompted at point of sale
- Review exchange rates before large purchases
