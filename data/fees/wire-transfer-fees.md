# Wire Transfer Fees

Wire transfers move funds electronically between financial institutions.

## Domestic Wire Transfers
- Outgoing: $25 per transfer
- Incoming: $15 per transfer
- Processing window: same business day if submitted before 3:00 PM

## International Wire Transfers
- Outgoing: $45 per transfer
- Incoming: $20 per transfer
- Processing time: 2-5 business days
- Intermediary bank fees may apply

## Required Information
For outgoing wires you need the recipient's name, account number, routing number (or SWIFT code for international), and amount.

## Cancellation
A wire can be cancelled only if it has not yet been processed. Once sent, wires are final.
