# Fee Waiver Policy

Certain fees can be waived based on account type or relationship balance.

## Automatic Waivers
- Premium checking: no monthly fee with $10,000 combined balance
- Standard checking: no monthly fee with direct deposit or $1,500 minimum balance
- Savings: no monthly fee with $300 minimum balance

## Relationship Waivers
Maintain a combined balance of $25,000 or more across deposit and investment accounts to receive:
- Free overdraft protection transfers
- Two free wire transfers per month
- Free stop payments

## Requesting a One-Time Waiver
Contact customer service within 30 days of the fee. One-time waivers are granted at our discretion for good-standing accounts.
