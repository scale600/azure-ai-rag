# Overdraft Fees and Protection

Overdrafts occur when you spend more than your available balance.

## How Overdraft Works
When a transaction exceeds your available balance, we may either pay it (overdraft) or return it (insufficient funds). Each paid overdraft item incurs a $35 fee.

## Overdraft Protection Options
1. Link a savings account: funds transfer automatically, $12 per transfer
2. Link a credit card: cash advance terms apply
3. Opt out: transactions are simply declined with no fee

## Avoiding Overdraft Fees
- Set up low-balance alerts in online banking
- Keep a buffer balance
- Review pending transactions regularly

## Refund Requests
Overdraft fees may be refunded in certain circumstances, such as bank error or first-time occurrences. Contact customer service to request a review.
