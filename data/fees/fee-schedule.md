# Fee Schedule

Our fee schedule lists all standard service charges. Fees are subject to change with 30 days' notice.

## Monthly Maintenance Fee
- Standard checking: $0 (waived with direct deposit or $1,500 minimum daily balance)
- Premium checking: $12/month (waived with $10,000 combined balance)

## Overdraft Fee
- $35 per overdraft item, up to 3 items per day
- Overdraft protection transfer: $12 per transfer
- Overdraft grace: transactions under $5 are not charged

## ATM Fees
- In-network ATMs: free
- Out-of-network ATM: $3 per withdrawal
- International ATM: $5 plus 1% foreign transaction fee

## Other Fees
- Stop payment: $30
- Returned deposit: $12
- Wire transfer (domestic): $25 outgoing, $15 incoming
